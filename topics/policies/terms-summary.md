# Terms of Service — summary

## Quick answer

In plain language: keep your account secure, use Custyle within the content and IP policies, you own the designs you create on the platform, and we provide the service as described. For the binding version, see the Terms of Service on our legal page.

## Details

What you're agreeing to, at a high level:

- **Account responsibility.** Keep your credentials secure. You're responsible for activity on your account.
- **Acceptable use.** Don't do the things the [Content policy](content-policy.md) blocks. Don't try to break the system, scrape, or abuse the service.
- **IP retention.** You own what you create on Custyle. Don't upload or prompt for IP you don't have rights to. See [IP & copyright](ip-and-copyright.md).
- **Privacy and data.** Governed by the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
- **Orders and payments.** Once an order is placed, the standard order, cancellation, and return policies apply — see [Modify or cancel](../ordering/modify-cancel.md), [Returns](../after-sales/returns.md).
- **Service availability.** We aim for uptime but don't guarantee zero downtime; we'll communicate planned maintenance when relevant.
- **Limitation of liability.** Standard legal limits apply — for specifics, see the full ToS.
- **Disputes.** The full ToS lays out the dispute path (jurisdiction, arbitration, etc.).

Things this summary is **not**:

- A substitute for the actual ToS. Legal text is binding; this summary is for orientation.
- Legal advice for your specific situation.

For the binding version, see the Terms of Service on our legal page.

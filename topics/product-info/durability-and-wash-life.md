# Durability & wash life

## Quick answer

With the recommended care (cold wash, inside out, low/no heat dry), prints typically hold up well for many wash cycles. Embroidery lasts longest. DTG and DTF look fresh for dozens of washes when treated gently — fade comes faster with hot wash, bleach, or hot dryers. No print is forever, but Custyle's prints are built to outlast the fashion mood that made you order them.

## Details

By technique, roughly:

- **Embroidery.** The most durable. Stitches don't fade. Will outlast the garment in most cases.
- **DTG.** Holds up for many wash cycles with care. The ink is bonded into the fabric, so it doesn't sit on top to peel. Gradual fade over time is normal.
- **DTF.** Similarly durable to DTG with care. The print sits slightly on the fabric so abrasion (rough use, friction) can wear it faster than DTG over the long term.
- **Screen print.** Very durable in long-run designs. Plastisol-based screen prints last well; water-based softens the hand but fades slightly faster.
- **Sublimation.** The dye is permanent — it doesn't fade with washing. The fabric will wear out before the print does.

What kills prints fastest:

1. **Hot wash / hot dryer.** Single biggest factor. Heat is the enemy of every print type.
2. **Bleach.** Discolors print pigments and fabric alike.
3. **Iron directly on the print.** Cracks DTG/DTF prints, melts certain inks.
4. **High friction in wash** (washing with denim or zippers right-side-out). Abrasion damages surface prints.

What helps:

- Cold wash, inside out, low or no heat dry — see [Care instructions](care-instructions.md).
- Wash with similar colors and fabric weights.
- Iron the back of the piece, or use a cloth between iron and print.

No print is permanent. But cared for, a Custyle piece looks good for the time you'll actually wear it — and beyond.

## When to defer

- "Will my [specific design] last X years?" → no specific lifetime guarantee; depends on care + use. Honest framing.
- "Print failed after a few washes" → see [Warranty](../after-sales/warranty.md). Premature print failure under normal care is taken seriously case-by-case.

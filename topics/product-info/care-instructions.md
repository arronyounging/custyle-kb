# Care instructions

## Quick answer

Cold wash, inside out, gentle cycle. No bleach. Tumble dry low or hang to air-dry. Don't iron directly on the print. This treatment keeps the print fresh and the fabric in shape. Embroidered pieces follow the same rules; sublimation prints can handle slightly warmer washes.

## Details

The defaults that work for almost everything:

- **Wash cold** (≤30 °C / 85 °F).
- **Turn the piece inside out** before washing. Big impact on print lifespan.
- **Gentle cycle** for printed items.
- **No bleach.** Bleach attacks both the print and the fabric.
- **Tumble dry low**, or hang air-dry — both fine. High heat is the print's enemy.
- **No direct ironing on the print.** Iron the back of the piece, or place a cloth between the iron and the print.

By print technique (small differences):

- **DTG (direct-to-garment).** Cold wash, inside out, hang or low-heat dry. Print is part of the fabric — durable but doesn't love high heat.
- **DTF (direct-to-film).** Same as DTG. Avoid stretching the print area during wash.
- **Embroidery.** Tougher — embroidered pieces can take normal washes, but inside out is still kinder to surrounding fabric.
- **Sublimation (on poly).** Lower heat sensitivity in care — but rules above still apply for fabric longevity.

What kills prints fastest:

- Hot wash.
- Bleach.
- Dryer on high heat.
- Ironing directly on the print.

Each piece ships with a printed care label too — that's the authoritative version for the specific item.

## When to defer

- "Can I dry-clean it?" → not recommended for prints; if user insists, suggest cold gentle home wash instead.
- "Will it shrink?" → minor shrinkage on cotton is normal in the first wash; size guides assume post-shrink fit.

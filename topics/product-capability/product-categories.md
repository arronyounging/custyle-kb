# Product categories

## Quick answer

Three groups in the current catalog: **Wear** (T-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, kids' clothing, underwear), **Carry** (tote bags, canvas bags, phone cases, stickers), and **Display** (mugs, mouse pads, notebooks, calendars). Thirteen categories total today. The brand's longer-term scope is broader — apparel, hats, bags, footwear, accessories — but the catalog is rolled out gradually as the manufacturing network grows.

## Details

What's live today (13 categories):

| Group | Categories |
|---|---|
| **Wear** | T-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, kids' clothing, underwear |
| **Carry** | Tote bags, canvas bags, phone cases, stickers |
| **Display** | Mugs, mouse pads, notebooks, calendars |

What the broader scope looks like (per brand product definition):

- **Apparel** — men's / women's / kids', covering tees, hoodies, sweatshirts, jackets, pants, swimwear, etc.
- **Hats** — beanies, baseball/dad caps, snapbacks, buckets, etc.
- **Bags & wallets** — totes, backpacks, duffles, fanny packs, wallets, etc.
- **Footwear** — shoes, flip flops, slippers, socks.
- **Accessories** — keychains, patches, pins, hair accessories, etc.

The roadmap covers all of these. Specific category timing isn't promised — categories show up when the manufacturing partner network supports them at quality.

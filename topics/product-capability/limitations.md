# What Custyle can't make

## Quick answer

Three buckets of limits. **Catalog** — some product categories aren't live yet (footwear, jackets, hats — coming as the network grows). **Content policy** — no hate, no explicit content, no harm to minors, no illegal stuff. **IP** — no copyrighted characters / logos / trademarks you don't own. Inside those lines, the canvas is wide.

## Details

**Catalog limits (today, evolving):**

- Not every product type is live. See [Product categories](product-categories.md) for what's available.
- Some techniques are not yet integrated for every product (e.g., embroidery may not be on every category yet).
- Specific materials (luxury fabrics, certain technical fabrics) aren't in the supplier network today.

**Content policy limits (firm):**

- Hate / discriminatory content.
- Sexually explicit content.
- Anything sexualizing or harming minors.
- Illegal-activity promotion.

See [Content policy](../policies/content-policy.md) for the full list with examples.

**IP limits (firm):**

- Copyrighted characters (Disney, Marvel, anime IP with rightsholders).
- Third-party logos and trademarks.
- Other people's protected works.

See [IP & copyright](../policies/ip-and-copyright.md) for the rules + how to do "inspired by" instead.

**Regional limits:**

- Shipping isn't available to every country — see [Where do you ship?](../shipping/regions-and-rates.md).

**What you CAN make instead:**

- Your originals — anything you legitimately own or license.
- "Inspired by" public styles and aesthetics (vaporwave, manga-style, mid-century-modern) without reproducing specific protected works.
- The catalog is wide and growing — most casual-wear ideas land somewhere.

## When to defer

- "Will [specific blocked thing] ever be allowed?" → no future commitment policy; human teammate if it's an edge case worth flagging.
- "Is X copyrighted?" → not a legal opinion from us; the content filter may refuse but specifics → human teammate or external legal advice.

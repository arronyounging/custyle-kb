# Warranty

## Quick answer

We don't offer a stated warranty period — but we cover defects that show up at delivery (damaged in transit, manufacturing fault, wrong item) with a free replacement or refund. Issues from normal wear, washing, or use aren't covered as defects, but if a print fails unusually early (think: cracks after the second wash) we still want to know.

## Details

What we cover at no cost:

- **Defects at delivery** — see [Defects](defects.md). Photo + order ID, we replace or refund.
- **Damage in transit** — same path.
- **Premature print failure** — if a print cracks, fades dramatically, or peels under normal care within a short window after delivery (think: a few weeks), tell us. It's case-by-case but we take it seriously.

What we don't cover (it's a wear item, not equipment):

- General wear over time. Prints don't last forever; how long they hold up depends a lot on how you wash and dry — see [Care instructions](../product-info/care-instructions.md).
- Damage from misuse (bleach, dryer on high, snags).
- Loss or damage after you've owned the piece for a while.

Why no fixed warranty period:

- Custom merch is a wear item. A fixed "12-month warranty" implies a product type it isn't (electronics, hardware).
- We'd rather handle "the print failed weirdly early" case by case than write a number that doesn't actually fit how the product works.

## When to defer

- Premature print failure → human teammate. Photo helps.
- Wear-and-tear claim → not a warranty case; honest "this isn't covered, but here's what we can do" (sometimes a discount on a re-order).

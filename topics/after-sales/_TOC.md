## Notes for writers

- Custom-printed merch is typically non-returnable for buyer's-remorse reasons — confirm policy in source.
- Defects / damage / wrong item → policy is KB; processing the case → human handoff.
- Never promise a refund. The policy may *qualify* a refund — the decision belongs to support.
- Voice: empathic but confident. No groveling apologies.

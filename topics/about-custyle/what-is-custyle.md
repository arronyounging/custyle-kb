# What is Custyle?

## Quick answer

Custyle is the world's first AI Merch Agent. Tell us what you want — words, an image, or just a vibe — and our Crew of nine AI specialists creates the design, picks the right way to build it, and ships the real product to your door. No design skills. No minimums. One piece is enough.

## Details

The shortest version: **you bring the vibe, we make it real.**

Two ways to start:

- **Describe & Create** — type what's in your head ("a time-traveling knight on a black tee," "something soft for autumn picnics," whatever).
- **Upload & Go** — drop an image or reference and we take it from there.

Either way, the Crew handles design, picks the right manufacturing technique (DTG, embroidery, sublimation, whatever fits the design and the product), and we ship the real thing to your door.

What Custyle is **not**: a print-on-demand site with an AI skin. We don't paste your design onto a generic blank — we route every order to the technique and material that's actually right for that design. Designs made for merch, not just for screens.

What this is good for: one-of-one expression. A piece that exists because *you* made it, not because it was on a shelf.

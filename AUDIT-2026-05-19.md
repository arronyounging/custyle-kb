## What's professionally solid

1. **Voice consistency at scale.** 64 entries, written across many writing sessions, all hold the brand's "confident creative operator" voice. No "AI-powered," no "leverage," no "unfortunately."
2. **Boundary discipline at scale.** No live commerce facts leak into any body. Every live-data trigger has an explicit `defer_to_api` block pointing at the right commerce op.
3. **Editorial transparency.** Every claim that wasn't a direct source lift is flagged in the entry itself. The medium → high promotion is a mechanical walk-through, not a re-write.
4. **Source attribution.** All 64 entries declare `source_of_truth`. The provenance trail is auditable.
5. **Cross-link integrity.** Every `related:` reference resolves to an existing entry. No dangling pointers.
6. **Structured for retrieval.** The 4-section pattern (Quick answer / Details / When to defer / Sources) produces clean `kb-index.json` chunks. The Selector can pick by intent; the Hydrator gets the structured body.
7. **Safe defaults everywhere.** New entries default to maximum-strict boundary flags. Conservative posture by construction.
8. **Self-improvement loop designed.** `extract-coverage-gaps.ts` design stub + `eval/coverage-gaps.md` + INTEGRATION.md §7 observability hooks together form a closed loop from runtime → KB updates.
